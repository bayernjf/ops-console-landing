# ops-console

> The internal operations console of the Tier 0 product family: agents execute
> upstream, humans sign off on the holds. ops-console is the post-login workbench
> where internal operations run. It is not merchant-facing, has no open
> registration, and is not a SaaS.

## What the workbench does

1. **Minimal session, no user table** — a single environment password plus an
   HMAC-signed cookie; two roles (owner / operator); no account table, no
   registration, no password-reset flow; zero upstream keys in the browser.
2. **Approval workbench (D1–D3 queue)** — held items queue as cards; approve or
   reject; results flow back consistently through the zeus `/approval` endpoint,
   the same channel agents wait on; the queue refreshes on a 20s poll.
3. **Read-only business dashboard** — reuses the commerce dashboard: orders,
   refunds, content and net amount; no cost data. The console reads; upstream
   services decide and write.
4. **Webhook card notifications** — every hold arrives as a webhook card in the
   IM client carrying an approval deep link; IM only notifies (Feishu / WeChat /
   email) — approve and reject never happen inside the IM client.
5. **Controlled, role-gated writes** — D3 listing changes are owner-only; D2
   refunds are open to both roles; manual stock adjustments where the flow
   allows; every write executes through commerce endpoints.

## Technical form

Single-process Astro 7 SSR (node standalone) with React 19 interactive islands.
One process = one deployment unit. Upstream bearer tokens live server-side only.
Shutting the console down does not affect the underlying zeus and commerce APIs —
the console is a lens on upstream services, not a dependency of them.

## Design principles

- **One process = one deployment unit**: one config, one restart, one thing to
  reason about.
- **Tokens stay server-side**: the browser holds only an HMAC cookie; there is no
  path for the client to call zeus or commerce directly.
- **Blast radius is the UI**: the workbench must never become a system the
  operations loop depends on.
